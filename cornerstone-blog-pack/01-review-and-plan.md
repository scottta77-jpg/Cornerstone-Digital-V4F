# Cornerstone Digital: blog review and replacement plan

Research checked 21 October 2026.

## Recommendation

Keep the commercial themes and dark visual direction. Rewrite the articles before publishing. The supplied plan has useful subject choices, but weak evidence, repeated sales language, overlapping topics and claims that exceed what SEO, automation or AI search can guarantee.

The replacement pack contains nine complete articles and nine original thumbnails, covering the actual live listing: one featured article and eight cards. It keeps the agency's Design & Build, Strategy & Consulting, Technology and Growth positioning and addresses owners of small service businesses.

## Live-site findings to address during publishing

On 21 October, `/blogs/` displayed nine templates. Direct HTTP checks confirmed that the sampled Minimalist Branding and Designing for Accessibility routes redirect to trailing-slash URLs and return HTTP 200. The featured View Details link uses `href="#"`, so it does not open an article. Replace that placeholder and verify every card after publishing.

The Minimalist Branding article also contains a body about typography and global brand recall. Replace the body, related links and metadata together so the headline and content describe the same subject.

The listing also retained template footer/navigation language, including “Blog Details”, “Work Details” and design-only newsletter copy. Replace that surrounding copy as part of the blog overhaul. Do not remove any attribution that a template licence requires without checking the licence.

The homepage contains the named team profiles and client automation descriptions used in the draft. Earlier context does not independently verify these implementations or quantified outcomes. Avoid expanding them into case studies without delivery records, client approval and a measurement period. The footer location is also inconsistent with the established Brentwood/Essex context; use accurate business details rather than inventing an office address.

The supplied attachment describes sidebar metadata and a main content column. The live full-post sample confirms the Subject, Read Time and Author fields followed by the article body. The pack preserves those fields and uses short sections, tables and ordinary lists. Check the final responsive CMS rendering when inserting the new content.

## Publishing sequence and internal links

Create all nine destinations before enabling cross-links. Replace the templates together once the routes work; this removes placeholder content and avoids linking readers into missing new articles. If the CMS needs a staggered rollout, keep links to unpublished articles disabled until their destinations exist.

Use these priority groups to guide implementation effort: 01/02/03 first for buying and enquiry decisions; 04/05/07 for visibility and follow-up; 06/08/09 for technical reassurance and approach selection. This is an implementation order, not a scheduled automation.

Every article includes a contextual CTA using the existing `https://cornerstonedg.com/#booking` destination. Add links to genuine corresponding service pages when available. Do not invent service-page URLs to fill a checklist. The pack provides related-post mappings for the CMS and contextual links where they fit naturally.

For old URLs with real links or traffic, choose a genuinely relevant replacement and use an appropriate permanent redirect. Do not send every removed post to the homepage. If a URL has no equivalent, handle its removal deliberately rather than creating misleading redirects.

## SEO, AEO and GEO implementation

Each article includes a distinct SEO title, description, slug, excerpt, image description and primary query. The title answers a recognisable customer problem. The opening gives a short answer where useful; its length is editorial, not a supposed algorithmic requirement.

Use one page H1 and a logical heading hierarchy. Ensure each article is a working, indexable page with a suitable canonical URL, visible content, ordinary crawlable links and inclusion in the intended sitemap. Check existing indexing controls before changing them.

Use accurate BlogPosting structured data with the visible headline, organisation author and publisher. Populate real publication and modification dates at publishing; do not assign October 2026 to an unpublished article. Add the final public image URL after uploading the asset. A schema blueprint is provided separately. Structured data helps describe content; it does not guarantee a rich result or AI citation. [Google Article documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article).

The AI-search guide should be reviewed against current [Google guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) when maintained. Useful original content, discoverability and accurate business information matter more than rigid answer lengths or special files. Do not manufacture city pages, staff biographies, awards or client outcomes to create apparent authority.

## Thumbnail system

Nine individually generated conceptual illustrations share charcoal, yellow and olive accents. Each article has a distinct subject. They contain no headline text, fabricated analytics or screenshots presented as real client systems.

The pack includes original PNGs and WebP exports that preserve the artwork. Use the WebP files for publishing and adapt responsive sizes to the CMS. Reserve image dimensions to avoid layout movement. If a thumbnail is decorative beside the same linked title, an empty alt attribute may be more appropriate than repeating the supplied descriptive alt text. If it is the only link, give it an accessible destination name. [W3C image guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/).

## Measurement and the next evidence to add

Record the launch date and preserve the pre-launch baseline. Check working pages, indexing, form delivery and analytics first. Review impressions, query relevance, clicks and qualified enquiries after enough data accumulates. Compare 30-, 60- and 90-day views as useful checkpoints, accounting for seasonality and small samples rather than treating those intervals as promised growth deadlines.

For AI visibility, record platform, date, exact prompt, sources cited and any measurable referral. Treat repeated prompt checks as a limited observation sample. Connect website activity to enquiry quality and sales where possible.

The next improvement should be first-hand evidence: one consented client walkthrough with actual screenshots, the original brief, verified delivery details and dated measurements. A Waddat wholesale-enquiry journey or a Wellington coaching-enquiry walkthrough could become a useful case study once the current implementation and permissions are checked. Do not attach conversion claims without the underlying data.
