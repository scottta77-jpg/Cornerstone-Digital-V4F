# Research and editorial notes

Research checked 21 October 2026. This series adds ten articles to the existing nine; it does not replace them.

## How the topics were selected

The original collection covers website enquiries, cost, AI phone handling, local/AI search, introductory automation, performance, reviews, accessibility and build approaches. The new collection moves into more specific buying and operational questions rather than rewriting those same introductions.

The audience remains owners and managers of small service businesses, particularly in Essex, London and surrounding operating areas. The series supports Cornerstone Digital's Design & Build, Strategy & Consulting, Technology and Growth services.

Public competitor pages show topics and formats, not reliable per-post traffic or conversion figures. The sources below are a content benchmark, not a list of independently verified highest-performing articles. No competitor's outcomes have been presented as Cornerstone results. Editorial query targets are supplied without invented search volumes or ranking forecasts.

## Editorial and search choices

Each article has a distinct title, query focus, excerpt, SEO title, description and proposed slug. Short opening answers, descriptive headings, useful tables and a clear next step support readers comparing services. These are editorial choices, not guarantees of AI selection.

The calendar balances visibility, conversion, operations, technology, evidence and planning. The Christmas article is placed before Christmas, and the 90-day plan is placed at year end. All ten Monday dates are retained, including 28 December.

Contextual internal links point to the original collection or earlier weeks in the new series. The supplied reference file maps the earlier nine posts. Antigravity must confirm those destinations exist on the actual site and map any changed slugs. Future posts must not appear as live links before publication.

Maintain the organisation byline. Add a named author only where that person is genuine and accepts the attribution. Publish actual dates, supported claims and accurate structured data. FAQ rich-result promises, fabricated results and keyword-only location pages remain excluded.

Before each release, recheck any platform-specific instruction if the service has changed since this research date. Review source availability, the final rendered page and the working booking destination.
